Turn the photos you only took because of the colours into paint-chip swatch cards. [Open it](days/day-07/site/index.html)

- Drop, paste or choose photos. Each one becomes a card with its palette pulled out automatically.
- The palette favours the colours that *stand out*, not just the most common ones: k-means in Lab colour space, then clusters are ranked by size and vividness and kept visibly distinct.
- **Click the photo** to add the colour under your cursor (a loupe shows it first). **Click a band**, then the photo, to swap that colour for another spot.
- Every colour gets a paint-store-style name worked out from the colour itself ("Dusty Rose", "Deep Teal", "Bone", "Rust"). Names and hex codes are editable.
- Three layouts: **Tall**, **Wide**, and **Chips only** (a long paint strip with a tiny photo stamp). Slider for 2–9 colours; **Shuffle** tries another set.
- **Download PNG** exports the card at full size (1200×1800 or 1800×1200).
- Cards are saved in your browser (IndexedDB) and shown on a shelf, so they're still there next time. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- iPhone HEIC photos only open in Safari; in other browsers use JPG or PNG.
