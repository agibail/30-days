A security-camera wall of live underwater feeds. [Open the monitors](days/day-02/site/index.html)

- 16 live cams: kelp forest, open sea, sharks and jellies at Monterey Bay Aquarium; five reef cams at Utopia Village in Utila, Honduras; Grand Cayman ReefCam; the Catalina Island marine reserve; and the manatee spring at Silver Springs, Florida
- 2×2 / 3×3 / 4×4 grids, click a feed to go full screen, ←/→ to flip cameras
- **Tour** mode cycles every camera like a guard's monitor (15s each)
- Looks: night-vision green, color, B/W, or clean
- On-screen display shows each camera's local time and coordinates; feeds that go down show static and NO SIGNAL
- Sound only plays for the camera in full view

Built with the YouTube IFrame API. The streams come from Monterey Bay Aquarium and explore.org's YouTube channels. If a stream ends and gets a new ID, swap it in the `CAMS` list.
