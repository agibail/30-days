A pretend label maker for my label art. I keep running out of real label tape, so this is where I type out labels and save them until I can print them for real. [Open the label maker](days/day-04/site/index.html)

- Type on the machine's keys (or your own keyboard) and hit **PRINT**. The tape feeds out of the slot step by step, the cutter chomps, and the label drops onto the yellow wall.
- **Drag labels** anywhere on the yellow wall to stick them. Drag one to the *peel off* circle (bottom left) to remove it. Double-click a stuck label to load it back into the machine.
- **TAPE** cycles cassettes: white, black, clear, pink, mint, silver, plus classic embossed tape (red, black, blue, green) with raised, slightly crooked white letters. **FONT** cycles typefaces, **Aa** toggles caps.
- **Tape box** (top right) keeps every label you print or **SAVE**. Tick *printed IRL* once you've made the real one. *Copy "to print" list* gives you the list to work through when you buy more tape. The LCD estimates the tape length in mm.
- Sounds are made in the browser with Web Audio (♪ button to mute). Everything is saved in the browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded.
- On a phone the tape comes out of the top of the machine, like a handheld.
