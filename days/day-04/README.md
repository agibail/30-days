A pretend label maker for my label art. I keep running out of real label tape, so this is where I type out labels and save them until I can print them for real. [Open the label maker](days/day-04/site/index.html)

The machine is modelled on a white desktop P-touch (the D410), without the logos: a raised tape compartment with the grey cutter lever on top, a grey dot-matrix LCD, grey function keys, an arrow pad, and the tape coming out of the left side.

- Type on the machine's keys (or your own keyboard) and hit **Print** (or Enter / OK). The tape feeds out step by step, the cutter chomps, and the label drops onto the yellow wall.
- **Drag labels** anywhere on the yellow wall to stick them. Drag one to the *peel off* circle (bottom left) to remove it. Double-click a stuck label to load it back into the machine.
- **Label** (or ▲ ▼) changes the tape: white, black, clear, pink, mint, silver, plus classic embossed tape (red, black, blue, green) with raised, slightly crooked white letters. **Font** cycles typefaces, **Frame** adds a border, **Caps** / **Shift** for case.
- **Symbol** inserts ★. Press it again to cycle ♥ ✓ ☺ ✿ ♪ … **Accent** turns the letter before the cursor into é, ñ, ç and so on. **Auto Format** picks a random tape, font and frame.
- ◀ ▶ move the cursor. **Clear** / **Esc** wipe the text. The magnifier key (**Feed**) scrolls a preview across the LCD. **Menu** turns the sound on or off. The power key switches the screen off and on.
- **Tape box** (top right) keeps every label you print or save with **File**. Tick *printed IRL* once you've made the real one. *Copy "to print" list* gives you the list to work through when you buy more tape. The LCD shows the tape length in inches.
- Sounds are made in the browser with Web Audio. Everything is saved in the browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded.
- On a phone the tape comes out of the top of the machine.
