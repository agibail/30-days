A new shade of yellow every day, each with a fun fact. [Open it](days/day-05/site/index.html)

- The whole page turns that day's yellow, and a paint-store-style chip shows its name, hex and RGB. Tap a code to copy it.
- **← Yesterday / Tomorrow →** (or the arrow keys) step through other days. A countdown shows when the next yellow arrives at midnight.
- The **fan deck** at the bottom holds all 34 shades: pigments (Indian Yellow, Gamboge, Orpiment, Chrome, Cadmium), food (Saffron, Turmeric, Butter, Egg Yolk), and pop culture (School Bus, Taxi, Simpsons, Post-it, Optic Yellow tennis balls).
- The shades are shuffled once with a fixed seed and assigned by date, so everyone sees the same yellow on the same day and the list repeats every 34 days.
